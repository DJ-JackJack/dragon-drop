# Installing Dragon Drop (for playtesters)

Version 0.1.13. You need a Windows 10 or 11 PC. It takes about five minutes.

## 1. Download it

1. Go to **https://github.com/DJ-JackJack/dragon-drop/releases/latest**
2. Scroll down to **Assets** and click **Dragon-Drop-Setup-0.1.13.exe** (about 120 MB).
3. If your browser says the file "isn't commonly downloaded": in Edge, click the **…** next to it, then **Keep**, then **Show more** and **Keep anyway**. (Dragon Drop isn't signed with a paid certificate yet, so browsers and Windows don't recognise it.)

## 2. Install it

1. Open the file you downloaded.
2. Windows says **"Windows protected your PC"**. Click **More info**, then **Run anyway**. Only do this for the file from the page above.
3. It installs in a few seconds, with no questions, and starts. Afterwards you'll find **Dragon Drop** on your desktop and in the Start menu.

## 3. First start

1. It asks **"Where are your maps?"** Click **Start fresh**. Your maps will be kept in `Documents\Dragon Drop\projects`.
2. It opens **First Map**, a made-up world to try things on.
3. Close the window to quit. It saves as you go, and once more as it closes.

## 4. Optional: your asset packs

If you own map asset packs (folders of pictures, .zip files or other map makers' pack files), click **Settings** (top right) → **Maps and assets** → **Choose folder…** under **Asset library**, and pick the folder that holds them. They're read where they are and never copied; a big library takes a minute or two to read the first time.

Without packs you still get Dragon Drop's own **Starter Pack**: ten free floor textures.

## 5. Optional: the AI assistant

The panel on the right is an AI assistant that can build maps with you. You don't need it to test most of Dragon Drop, and it has its own cost:

- **Claude Code** (the default) needs a paid Claude plan (Pro or Max). The first time, the panel shows **Install Claude Code for me**: click it, wait about a minute, then sign in with your Claude account in the browser window that opens.
- **Codex** works with any ChatGPT account, the free one too: choose it in **Settings** → **AI assistant**, then sign in when it asks.
- To skip it, click **Claude** in the top bar to hide the panel.

## 6. Updates

Dragon Drop checks for a new version by itself. When one is ready it asks **Restart now** or **Later**. Your maps and settings are never touched by an update.

## If something goes wrong

- **Settings** → **Help and updates** has **Questions and answers** and **Ask a question or report a problem**.
- Or tell whoever sent you this. Include what you did, what you expected and what you saw, plus a screenshot if you can. If it crashed or won't start, also click **Open logs** at the bottom of Settings and send the file `server.log`.
- Never send your access key or an API key to anyone.

## The playtest form

You'll get a form, **Dragon Drop 0.1.13 Playtest.pdf**. Open it in Edge (or any PDF reader that fills in forms), click in the boxes to type and on the circles to choose, then save it with **Ctrl+S** and send it back. Skip anything you don't get to.

Before trying something big on a map you care about, make a copy first: **Map panel** → **Other maps** → **Duplicate this map**.
