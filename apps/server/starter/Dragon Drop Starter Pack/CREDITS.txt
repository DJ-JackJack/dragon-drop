Dragon Drop Starter Pack
========================

A few floor textures and objects that come with Dragon Drop, so there is something to paint and
furnish battlemaps with before you add asset packs of your own.

Floors
------
Every picture in Floors/ is from Poly Haven (https://polyhaven.com) and is released under CC0 1.0
(public domain dedication, https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/): free for any use,
commercial included, with no credit required. Poly Haven's licence page: https://polyhaven.com/license
We credit the artists anyway, with thanks.

What was changed: the 1k colour picture of each texture was renamed, recompressed as JPEG, and six
of them (marked *) were halved to 512 pixels so one repeat covers 2 squares, near their real size.
Gravel was made darker and greyer, with more contrast, and Forest Floor's bare soil darker, so their
small swatches in the pickers read as what they are.

in Dragon Drop's source) and are also given to the public domain under CC0 1.0: free for any use,
commercial included, with no credit required. 256 pixels are one grid square.
